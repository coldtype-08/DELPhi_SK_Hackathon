Harold Jessup, MD, Magnolia Grove Clinic, Jackson, MS (AR)

> Estimated that two of every three older patients need a pharmacist review before a change is made, which adds about a week to the decision.
> Request logged: caregiver-facing education on what to expect during a titration period, at label level. Routing to Medical Information.
