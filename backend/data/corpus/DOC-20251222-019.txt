Interaction Note   |   Date: December 22, 2025   |   MSL: Priya Menon
HCP: Peter Kowalczyk, MD, Green Mountain Neuroscience, Burlington, VT (RSM)

Brief in-person visit at Green Mountain Neuroscience on the afternoon of December 22, arranged around a shortened holiday clinic schedule. I had offered to make this a phone call, but the office preferred to meet while I was already in Burlington.

The conversation was almost entirely logistical and concerned the spring regional epilepsy meeting. The practice expects to send two clinicians and one nurse practitioner, contingent on coverage; the difficulty is that the meeting falls on a Thursday and Friday, which are the two heaviest clinic days here. Registration is open, but the group rate at the conference hotel closes in mid-January and the travel budget has not yet been confirmed by the practice manager. I was asked, informally, which sessions tend to fill first and whether the poster hall justifies the extra day. I answered in general terms and made no commitments.

We also discussed who else from the region is likely to attend. Two colleagues from a neighboring practice are apparently going, and there was interest in coordinating ground travel from the airport.

No clinical or product topics were raised by either party, and no materials were requested or left. There were no safety items to report; I confirmed this before closing.

My follow-ups: (1) forward the published agenda once it is posted, (2) propose a first-quarter meeting date after the January clinic calendar is finalized, and (3) thank the coordinator who arranged this visit on short notice.
