Naomi Feldman, MD, Twin Rivers University Hospital, Columbus, OH (RSM)
> Meeting held on June 4 at Twin Rivers, approximately thirty-five minutes; third contact with this center since October 2025.
> The scheduled topic was the transition clinic, which now sees patients from age sixteen jointly with pediatric neurology twice a month.
> Raised, unprompted, that a 16-year-old with drug-resistant focal seizures had to stay on a failing regimen because cenobamate is not approved below 18.
> I confirmed that the XCOPRI indication in the United States is limited to adults and did not discuss use below that age; no request to do so was made. The point being made concerned the absence of an option and what is said to the family in the meantime.
> Follow-ups: (1) log as a recurring unmet need in the adolescent population, the fourth such account recorded this quarter, (2) ask Medical Information which published adolescent pharmacokinetic literature, if any, may be shared on request, (3) no safety event was described and nothing has been routed to safety.
