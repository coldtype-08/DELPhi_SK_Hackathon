Interaction Note   |   Date: July 8, 2026   |   MSL: Rachel Suh
HCP: Yolanda Reyes, MD, Copper Mesa Neurology, Mesa, AZ (RSM)

I met with the attending on the afternoon of July 8 at the Mesa office. The slot was only twenty minutes, but the conversation was unusually substantive and the physician did most of the talking.

The visit opened with an unprompted account of one patient. Described a drug-resistant adult who went from weekly seizures to rare breakthroughs after titration. The patient had cycled through several regimens over a number of years before being started on XCOPRI last winter, and what seemed to strike this prescriber most was that the change has held through the spring rather than fading after the first weeks. I recorded the account as a single individual experience, noted that no denominator was offered, and was careful neither to generalize from it nor to draw any comparison with other agents.

We then spent a few minutes on practical matters, namely the follow-up interval used for patients in that situation and how the office captures seizure counts between visits so that a change is not missed.

I was asked politely whether any published long-term data on refractory adults could be shared, and I said I would route the question to Medical Information rather than answer from memory. Nothing else was requested of me. The visit closed on the July heat and a flight out of Phoenix that had been delayed twice.
